What We Collect

Right now, this site stores your shopping cart in your own browser (not on a server) so it's there when you come back. When checkout is connected through Square, we'll also receive the order and shipping information needed to fulfill your purchase — Square's own privacy policy governs how your payment details are handled, since we never see or store full card numbers ourselves.

How We Use It

Order information is used only to fulfill, ship, and follow up on your purchase. We don't sell your information to third parties.

Cookies & Local Storage

This site uses your browser's local storage to remember your cart contents. It doesn't use tracking cookies of its own.
